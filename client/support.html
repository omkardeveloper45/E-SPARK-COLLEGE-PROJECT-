Frequently Asked Questions

⚡ How do I book an EV charging slot?

Go to "Find Station", select your nearest charging station, choose connector plug type & date time slot, and click "Book Slot Now".

💳 What payment methods are supported?

We support UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), Credit & Debit cards, Net Banking, E-SPARK Wallet, and cash payments at station kiosks.

📄 How do I get my Tax Invoice?

After payment completion, you are automatically redirected to the Tax Invoice page where you can print or download the PDF.

🔴 Can I stop charging anytime?

Yes! Visit "Live Telemetry" page and click "Stop Charging" to terminate energy transfer safely and proceed to final billing.
